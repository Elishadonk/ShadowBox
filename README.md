# Cloude Pay — Wallet & C1 Coin

**Cloude Pay is a digital wallet project being built to make everyday digital finance and payments easier to access.** The app brings together a Nigerian naira (NGN) wallet, a C1 Coin wallet, transaction activity, and Quick Mine rewards in one mobile experience.

### The vision for C1 Coin

C1 Coin is being developed as a utility-focused digital token for the Cloude Pay ecosystem. The goal is for C1 to become useful for everyday purchases—not simply a balance users hold in a wallet.

The first planned uses include **buying mobile data, airtime, and recharge cards through Cloude Pay**, as the necessary purchase integrations and services become available. As the ecosystem grows, the longer-term vision is to expand the Cloude Pay website into a marketplace where users may be able to spend C1 on digital products and, eventually, physical goods such as clothing and phones.

### Building real utility, step by step

The priority is to build practical reasons to use C1, grow adoption responsibly, and work toward a clearer, more reliable way for users to understand its value. C1's price is not fixed or guaranteed, and no exchange listing or investment return is promised. The purchasing and marketplace ideas above are **development goals, not a claim that every feature is already live**. Availability will depend on implementation, service partnerships, and applicable requirements.

## Overview

Shadow Box is a secure communication platform built with modern technologies to provide fast, reliable, and privacy-focused messaging and calling.

The project is designed with a local-first architecture and aims to evolve into a resilient communication platform capable of operating across diverse networking environments while maintaining security, performance, and a great user experience.
